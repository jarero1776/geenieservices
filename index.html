What We Do

Senior move management, in plain English.

Most people have not moved in thirty or forty years. A lifetime of belongings, a closing date, and a much smaller floor plan add up to a project no one should have to manage alone — especially not in the middle of a health change or a family decision that was hard to make in the first place.

That is the whole job. We start with what stays and what goes, handle the packing, line up and supervise the movers, then unpack and set up the new place so it feels like home the first night — not three weeks later. If there is a house full of furniture left behind, we plan the estate sale and clear the rest so you can close on schedule.

  • 01 Sorting and downsizingRoom by room, at a pace that leaves room for the stories — deciding what comes along, what goes to family, and what finds a new home.
  • 02 PackingCareful, clearly labeled packing, with the things you will want on night one kept separate and easy to find.
  • 03 Coordinating the moversWe book licensed, insured movers, confirm the details, and stay on site to direct the day so you never have to manage a crew.
  • 04 Unpacking and resettlingBeds made, kitchen usable, pictures on the walls. You walk into a home, not a stack of cartons.
  • 05 Estate sale planningWhat is left is sorted, priced, and sold — with donations and disposal arranged for everything that does not sell.

Services

Take the whole project, or just the part you need.

Every move is put together differently. Some families hand over the entire transition; others just need the downsizing done before the listing photos. Both are welcome.

Move planning & consultation

A walkthrough of the current home and the new floor plan, then a written plan and timeline covering what happens when — and who is handling it.

Downsizing & decluttering

Hands-on, patient sorting in short sessions so decisions never pile up. We measure what actually fits and help decide the rest without pressure.

Packing & unpacking

Fragile items wrapped properly, every box labeled by room and contents, and full unpacking on the other end — closets, kitchen, and all.

Estate sale coordination

Staging, pricing, and running the sale so the remaining furniture, china, tools, and collectibles bring back real value instead of becoming a burden.

Donation & disposal

Whatever is not kept or sold goes somewhere sensible. We arrange donation pickups with receipts for your records, plus recycling and responsible haul-away.

Home cleanout before closing

The house emptied, swept, and broom-clean for the buyer or the next tenant — coordinated around your closing date so nothing holds up the sale.

FAQ

Questions families ask first.

What exactly is senior move management?

It is having one experienced person run the entire transition out of a longtime home. A moving company loads a truck and drives it. A senior move manager starts weeks earlier — planning, sorting, and downsizing — then coordinates the movers, and stays until the new home is unpacked and livable and the old one is cleared out.

The point is to take the planning, the lifting, and the decision fatigue off the family, especially when adult children live out of the area or are managing this alongside jobs of their own.

Are you licensed, bonded, and insured?

Geenie Estate Services operates as a registered Washington business, and we are happy to walk you through our current credentials and coverage during your consultation — just ask, and we will put it in writing.

Any movers or haul-away companies brought onto your job are licensed and insured in their own right. We are glad to share who we are using before they arrive.

How does pricing work?

Every home is different, so we do not quote a number before seeing the situation. After the free walkthrough you will receive a written estimate covering the scope you actually want — whether that is the full move or just the downsizing.

You are never billed for the initial consultation, and you will always know the cost in writing before any work begins.

What happens to the items that are not kept?

Nothing worth something gets thrown out. Items generally go one of four ways: home with family members, into an estate sale, to a donation partner, or — only as a last resort — to recycling and disposal.

Donations go to local organizations and we collect the receipts for your tax records. You approve the plan for anything of real or sentimental value before it leaves the house.

Do you provide the movers, or hire them?

We hire and supervise them. Summer books licensed, insured local movers she trusts, confirms the arrangements, and is on site to direct the day — which means one person is accountable for how the move goes, not a handoff between vendors.

Donation pickups and junk haul-away work the same way: arranged and overseen by us, performed by vetted local partners.

How far ahead should we call?

Sooner is better — six to eight weeks before a move gives everyone breathing room, and starting earlier means decisions get made calmly rather than under a deadline.

That said, short-notice and urgent moves happen all the time, often after a fall or a hospital stay. Call anyway; we will tell you honestly what is possible in the time available.
